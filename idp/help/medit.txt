medit("names", Th, sol1, sol2, ..., parameters) : draws in the ffmedit window
  (load "medit").

  "names": one name per solution, separated by spaces. The 1D graph
  options go after a vertical bar:
    "u w | title | xlabel=... | ylabel=... | legend=a,b | logx | logy | loglog
     | xrange=a:b | yrange=a:b"
  A vector solution is written in brackets: [vx,vy] or [vx,vy,vz].

  Parameters:
    wait=true          the script waits for enter in the window;
                       wait=false: the script continues (animation)
    order=0            solution given by element (P0); 1 by default (vertices)
    bb=[[xmin,ymin],[xmax,ymax]]   fixed axes of the 1D graph
    meditff="prog"     ffmedit program to launch
    save="f.sol"       saves the solution to a file
    bin=1              binary transfer (default)

  Keys (the same as ffglut): enter next plot, p previous, Esc quit,
  ? help, f fill, o isolines, n N fewer/more isolines, m mesh,
  B boundary, l lighting, v palette, P value under the mouse, j axes, 3 2D relief,
  h H relief height, k complex part, s isosurfaces, V arrows,
  a A arrow length, e E exploded view, T normals, U overlay,
  F1 cutting plane, F2 move it, F3 fix it on the object, = initial view,
  c 2D cut u(x) / u(y), < > move it, shift + drag a segment,
  z Z zoom, w PPM capture, b black/white background. The rest: right button.
  1D graph: k lines/points, G grid, x y log, A fix the axes, i auto axes.

  Examples:
    medit("u", Th, u, wait=1);
    medit("u grad", Th3, u, [gx,gy,gz]);
    medit("p", Th, p, order=0);
    medit("u | Title | xlabel=x", ThL, u, bb=[[0,-1],[1,1]]);
