meshdual, meshdualL, meshdual3, meshdualS : the dual meshes of a mesh, meshL,
  mesh3 or meshS: one control volume around each vertex, the unknowns P1 of
  the finite volume schemes eq="...dm" of fvscheme. meshdual (2D) is the
  dual mesh of F. Bouchut, the others are median dual meshes.
  Example: meshdual Thstar = builddualmesh(Th); plot(Thstar, u[]);
  See help(builddualmesh), help(dualmeshcvarea).
