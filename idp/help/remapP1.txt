remapP1(Tho, uo[], Th, u[]) : conservative transfer of the P1 unknowns of
  the dual finite volumes (one value per vertex = the mean on its control
  volume) from Tho to Th (meshL, mesh): limited affine reconstruction
  (Barth-Jespersen) per control volume, integrated exactly on the new
  volumes (dual of F. Bouchut in 2D, median dual in 1D); sum |C_i| u_i
  conserved; several components together. For a P1 finite element
  (int u conserved): remapP1FE.
