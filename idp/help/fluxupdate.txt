dt = fluxupdate(FV, [u1, ...], [ur1, ...], [uBC1, ...]) : one evaluation of
  the finite volume scheme FV (see help("fvscheme")).

  [u...]   the unknowns (as many as the model, in its order); fluxupdate
           may correct them: vacuum (rho < 1e-13 -> 0), dry zones (H -> 0),
           tangent projection on meshS, mean on the periodic volumes
  [ur...]  output: ur = (1/|K|) sum on the faces of |f| * outgoing numerical
           flux (the topography source term included): the explicit
           Euler step is u[] -= dt*ur[] ; for eq="bbm" ur = R
  [uBC...] the outer state of the boundaries of type 0 (and 3) of labelBC (read
           in the boundary cell; on the dual mesh a mean of the vertices of the
           edge); to be given even without a Dirichlet condition (uBC = 0)
  return   the maximal stable time step for CFL = 1 (at most 1e4): take
           dt = CFL*fluxupdate(...) with CFL < 1

  The three arrays: same size, on the current mesh of the unknowns
  (after adaptmesh: ur = 0; uBC = ...;), ur initialized. If the unknowns are
  on a new mesh, the scheme is built again (rebuild=true) or an error
  is raised.
  Example: dt = CFL*fluxupdate(FV, [H,Hu,Hv,zb], [Hr,Hur,Hvr,zbr],
                                [HBC,HuBC,HvBC,zbBC]); H[] -= dt*Hr[];
