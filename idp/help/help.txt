help(function) or help("keyword") : help of a function or a keyword (in the
  kernel, no load; from a shell: ffhelp name). help() gives the list of the
  topics. For a function, help also prints its signatures (argument types) and
  its named parameters (name= type), even without a written help; for a type
  (mesh, meshL, matrix, ...), its members (Th.nv, Th[k].measure, ...).
  The written texts are the files help/<name>.txt of the include path
  (idp/help in the sources); a first line @other gives the help of other.
